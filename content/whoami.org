* the ∞-catgirl & witch apprentice

As of October 2026, this blog is run by *Roxanne Huang* (pronouns: she/they) (see below for more on names),
which is me, a graduate student in mathematics at Universität Regensburg,
trying to work her way into homotopy theory and algebraic K-theory.
I'm particularly interested in chromatic structures, equivariant and motivic homotopy theory.

I'm supported by the SFB1785, supervised by Prof. Marc Hoyois and Prof. Markus Land.
Previously I was a master student in the working group of Prof. Holger Reich at FU Berlin.

I enjoy reinterpreting and explaining well-known things,
as I slowly work towards the point where I'm able to produce something original.

** Names
- My name is *Roxanne Huang*, also sometimes *Ruochong Huang*.
- The blog is also run under the name *NaCNO*, a stylisation of a Japanese name *なつの*.
  The letter C therefore denotes a syllable ​/tsɯ/ and not ​the consonant /k/, although this
  choice is partially inspired by the (reconstructed) Old Chinese pronunciation of the
  first character of my personal name in Chinese.
